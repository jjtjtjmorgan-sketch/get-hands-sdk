# Community code of conduct

The Hands community is for people building useful, trustworthy developer tools together. Participation requires respectful, evidence-led collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be clear, kind, and constructive.
- Critique ideas, claims, and implementations—not people.
- Make room for different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Protect private data, credentials, identities, and unpublished security information.
- Give credit and respect software, content, and data licenses.
- Correct mistakes openly, including claims that overstate product maturity.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate disruption, credential exposure, attempts to bypass access controls, and publication of another person's private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

For a community-conduct concern, email [support@gethands.nl](mailto:support@gethands.nl) with **community conduct** in the subject. For a vulnerability or security incident, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, subject to safety and legal obligations.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents Hands in another community space.
